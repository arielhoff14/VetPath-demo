## Regional directory correction — November 15, 2026

The original demo imported only the Interview Tracker (five interviews, four hospitals), missing the workbook's **Pricing Comparison** and **Website Audit** tabs. The directory now imports all 28 regional source rows, deduplicated against the existing providers, plus the additional SF Veterinary Hospital listing: **29 provider records**. The unresolved Sacramento interview remains one record with an unverified branch/address; it is visible in the unfiltered directory but excluded from distance-based matches. This is coverage of the supplied directory, not a claim to list every Bay Area practice.

`data/pricing-comparison.csv` retains the 28 directory rows, including street addresses, phones and original source dates. `data/website-audit.csv` retains the corresponding capability and pricing evidence. Live sync fetches all three tabs before applying a single transaction. `directory_sources` retains the original directory/audit evidence separately from interview records. Staff overrides produce reviewable conflicts. Reimporting and restarting do not duplicate hospitals or reset patients, partnerships or availability.

The 27 identified spreadsheet addresses were checked against their branch sources. Twenty-five were rechecked against accessible official pages on November 15; Pacific Veterinary Emergency and Beacon's sites presented a challenge, so their contacts retain the spreadsheet's November 14 verification date rather than claiming a new verification. SAGE's shared page markup lists multiple branches: the record whose branch URL matches is used, not its shared Concord footer. Larkspur's VEG uses its published **Greenbrae 94904** postal address. Current identities include San Mateo Veterinary Emergency (formerly North Peninsula), VCA East Bay Veterinary Emergency, Omega Veterinary Group, and Solano-Napa's **4400 Central Place** location. SF Veterinary Hospital's official page confirms the previously missing **94110** ZIP.

`data/address-evidence.json` records the contact source, verification date, and coordinates for each identified spreadsheet branch: 22 Census address matches and five branch-specific SAGE structured-data coordinates. Census matches are street geocodes, not travel-time estimates. The geocoder checks street address and ZIP; a matched address is retained for review. If a subsequent address edit has no cached address match, its ZIP centroid is used and labeled approximate; if it cannot resolve, coordinates become unknown rather than retaining the previous location. Applying an address conflict updates coordinates and the generated database ZIP in the same transaction.

City and postal lookup uses the [GeoNames US postal dataset](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/US.zip), retrieved November 15, filtered by California county FIPS for all nine Bay Area counties plus Sacramento. The bundled **569 ZIP codes** include San Francisco's county name “City and County of San Francisco.” Attribution: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). City locations average postal coordinates unless a city-center override is recorded; municipalities sharing a postal city use their associated ZIP centroid. ZIP+4 and city/state/ZIP input resolve to the first five digits. These are approximate search origins, not validated mailing addresses or guaranteed exhaustive postal coverage. No paid geocoding key or third-party request is needed at search time.
